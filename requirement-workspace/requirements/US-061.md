## Observable scenarios

1. Ben loads an authorized trip: its overview combines trip summary,
   member/readiness counts and actual main-plan and split statuses.
2. Alice views a confirmed main plan with an unconfirmed split draft: setup,
   invitations, preferences, main plan, split, reunion and completion are
   distinguished as completed, current or upcoming, with split current. No
   next-action recommendation is shown.
3. Members follow available dashboard links or tabs to other pages without
   changing persisted state merely by navigating.
4. After a trip is completed, it is absent from the active-trip dashboard per
   the supplied draft's prior clarification; archive delivery is separate.
5. Signed-out and non-member callers receive the applicable existing access
   outcome without private trip information. No trips, missing data and read
   failures are distinguished from real trip state.
6. Every active-trip card shows the saved trip name, destination, start and end
   dates and opens the corresponding trip dashboard.
7. A shortcut to a not-yet-developed destination states that the destination is
   coming soon and does not navigate to a broken page.
8. Until the separate trip-details fix is delivered, opening dashboard
   navigation cannot trigger its automatic invitation issuance.

## Prior progress clarifications in the supplied draft

- Invitations are complete when the main plan is confirmed and joining closes;
  earlier invitation activity alone does not establish completion.
- While joining remains open, invitations is current and preferences is
  upcoming, even when a member has not submitted preferences.
- Once the main plan is confirmed and no split draft exists, the main-plan
  stage remains current. A cancelled split draft also returns the display to
  main plan current (user decisions, 2026-11-16).
- A confirmed split remains current while the trip is not completed (user
  decision, 2026-11-16). Reunion and completion remain upcoming until the
  completed trip leaves the active dashboard (user decision, 2026-11-16).
- The draft records a waiting message for a non-organizer who has not submitted
  preferences while invitations are current. No next-action recommendation
  is shown.
