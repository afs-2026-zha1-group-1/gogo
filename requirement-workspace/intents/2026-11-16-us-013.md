## Raw acceptance criteria (issue #9, governing)

**Required layers:** Frontend: Yes | Backend: Yes | Database: Yes (Read)

As a trip member, I would like to see who has joined and who is the organizer,
so that I know who is traveling with me and who manages shared decisions.

**Test context:** Synthetic Singapore Friends Day trip on 2026-12-05,
Asia/Singapore (+08:00). Alice is organizer; Ben, Chloe, and Daniel are members;
Eve is an outsider unless joining.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Given "Singapore Friends Day" contains Alice, Ben, Chloe, and Daniel, When Ben
  opens the member list, Then all four appear exactly once and only Alice is
  identified as organizer.
- Given Eve is not a member of "Singapore Friends Day", When she requests its
  member list, Then access is denied and no member identities are returned.

## Decisions taken at P1

| id | Decision | Evidence / rationale |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Expose `GET /api/trips/{id}/members` as a member-only read in the existing `trip` package. | Mirrors `GET /api/trips/{id}`; smallest blast radius; no new capability package. |
| D2 | Success body is `{ "tripId": <id>, "members": [{ "userId", "displayName", "organizer" }] }`, ordered by ascending `userId`. | Deterministic tests; per-row organizer flag satisfies "only Alice is identified as organizer" without a second field. Field names become contractual at P2. |
| D3 | The organizer row is included in the list; membership comes from `trip_member` joined to `app_user`; `organizer` is derived by comparing `userId` with `trip.organizer_id`. | Issue AC lists all four and calls Alice organizer; no new column or write path. |
| D4 | Denial precedence is identity → trip existence → membership, matching `TripController.read`. | Keeps the existing `trips` error contract; Eve with an unknown trip sees 404, not 403. |
| D5 | The frontend keeps `#/members` as the 成员 tab target and resolves the most recently opened trip in the app session; with no trip selected it shows a guidance state. | The shell contract fixes the route; no list-all endpoint exists (US-064 non-goal). A durable selection memory is a P3 UX choice, not a contract field. |
| D6 | The member screen renders only names, an avatar initial, the organizer badge and a member count; it does not read or display readiness, preference or generation data. | Issue design scope; prevents Screen 7 scope creep. |
| D7 | Modify the `app-shell` "Placeholder tabs" requirement so 成员 renders the real list while 分队 stays a placeholder. | Living Spec authority; US-064's placeholder was explicitly a pre-US-013 state. |
